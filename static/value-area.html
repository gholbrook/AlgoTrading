How the test works

At an eligible candle close, build a profile from the configured number of trailing candles. Require the current candle to satisfy the containment rule. Freeze VAL, VAH and POC, then observe subsequent candles. A confirmed exit requires more consecutive outside candles than the allowance.

Each profile distributes candle volume uniformly across its high–low prices. Equal allocation measures half the requested volume on each side of the POC and skips profiles with too little volume on either side. Formation windows with gaps or zero volume are skipped.

Earlier candles can warm the profile before the start date. Future observations stop at the end date. By default, the next observation starts after the prior one finishes, although formation windows can overlap. A positive sampling interval allows overlapping observations.
